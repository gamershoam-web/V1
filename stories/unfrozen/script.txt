# UNFROZEN  (slug: unfrozen)
# Title line: "If your town froze your age forever on your 12th birthday..."
# Narrator: first person, Milo, 12. Fast, punchy, almost no pauses.
# Target: ~150-160s spoken, like the reference. Lines starting with # are notes, not narration.

# --- HOOK (result first, mid-action) ---
Everyone in my town stopped growing up. Everyone... except me.

# --- THE RULE ---
You see, in this town, when the big clock strikes twelve on your Freezing Day, you stop aging. Forever.

# --- SOCIETY EXAMPLES (each its own location) ---
So everybody picks their favorite age. My teacher froze at nine, so she'd never miss recess. The baker froze at twenty-five, and he's been baking the same bread for two hundred years. And the mayor froze at sixty, because he says old people win every argument.

There are no birthday parties, because nobody has birthdays. The hospital closed in 1824. And the mirror shop? Out of business. Why check your face if it never changes?

# --- MY FREEZING DAY ---
Today was my Freezing Day. I'm twelve, and I had it all planned. Twelve forever. Recess forever. No homework, no taxes, no wrinkles. Ever.

The whole town came to watch. Tick. Tick. DONG!

Everyone cheered. Confetti everywhere. But I felt... nothing.

The next morning, my pants were too short. By lunch, my voice cracked. By dinner, I had a mustache. A big one.

# --- BUT (pivot) ---
But in a town where nobody changes, changing is the worst thing you can do.

The kids at school screamed. "He's GROWING!" The baker wouldn't sell me bread. The mayor put up a sign: NO AGING ALLOWED.

# --- ESCALATION ---
So I tried everything. I sat in the freezer. Brrr! I hid under my bed for a week. Nope. Fifteen. I even begged the clock. It didn't answer. It's a clock.

Sixteen. Eighteen. Twenty-five. By Friday, I needed glasses. By Sunday, my back went: Crack!

# --- THEN I NOTICED SOMETHING (the world's own rule) ---
Then I noticed something. The clock's hands only moved when I moved. When I sat still, it stopped. When I ran, it spun.

So I climbed the tower. Inside, behind the gears, there was a wall of names. One name every hundred years. And at the very bottom... mine.

The clock doesn't freeze anybody. It steals their time, and it pours all of it into one kid. Every birthday this whole town skipped... was landing on me.

# --- THE WIN ---
So I grabbed the biggest gear, and I pulled. Creeeak... SNAP! BOOM!

The clock went quiet. Then, all over town, something weird happened. Everybody got one year older. At the same time.

The baker found his first gray hair, and cried. My teacher turned ten, and she was furious. And me? I shrank right back to twelve. Mustache gone. Poof!

# --- PAYOFF ---
That night, the town had its first birthday party in two hundred years. One giant cake, for everybody.

And the next morning, the hospital opened its doors. The first baby born in two hundred years.

She was perfect. Tiny hands. Tiny feet.

# --- BUTTON (reopens the loop, callback to "Tick. Tick. DONG!") ---
And when she opened her eyes... way up on the tower... the broken clock went: Tick.
