# Unfrozen ⏰

**Title line:** "If your town froze your age forever on your 12th birthday..."

**Premise:** In Milo's town, the clock tower freezes your age forever when it strikes twelve on
your "Freezing Day". Everyone picks their favorite age. On Milo's Freezing Day, nothing happens,
and then he starts aging fast. He finds out the clock doesn't freeze anyone: it steals the whole
town's time and pours it into one kid every hundred years. He breaks the clock, everyone ages one
year at once, he shrinks back to twelve, and the town has its first birthday party and first
baby in 200 years. Then the broken clock ticks again when the baby opens her eyes.

The full narration is in [`script.txt`](script.txt) (444 words, about 2 min 35 s).

## How it matches the reference video

The reference ("Unmasked") is a 2 min 40 s short with about 138 cuts, roughly one new shot
every 1.2 s, a continuous music bed and almost no pauses in the narration. This story uses the
same structure, with its own premise, characters and plot:

| Reference structure | Unfrozen |
|---|---|
| A town with a yearly tradition (Masking Day banner) | Freezing Day banner over the clock-tower square |
| Signs showing how the rule changed the town | "HOSPITAL - CLOSED SINCE 1824", "MIRRORS - OUT OF BUSINESS", "NO AGING ALLOWED" |
| Townspeople shaped by the rule | Teacher frozen at 9, baker at 25 for 200 years, mayor at 60 |
| One kid who is the odd one out | Milo, the only one who keeps aging |
| A wall that holds the secret (dated masks) | A wall of names inside the clock tower, one every 100 years |
| Crib ending that restarts the story | The first baby in 200 years, and the broken clock goes "Tick" |

## Locations

1. **Clock-tower square:** a cobblestone square, a tall stone clock tower with a big clock face, a "FREEZING DAY" banner, bunting and confetti.
2. **Classroom:** tiny desks, a teacher who looks younger than the students.
3. **Bakery:** loaves of bread on shelves, a "SINCE 1826" sign over the door.
4. **Main street:** the boarded-up hospital and the closed mirror shop.
5. **Milo's house:** a bedroom (hiding under the bed) and a kitchen (sitting in the freezer).
6. **Inside the clock tower:** giant gears, a stone wall of carved names and years, Milo's name at the bottom.
7. **Hospital:** doors opening in the morning light, a crib.

  each), about 1,200 for Milo's designs, about 1,200 for the three side characters, about 400
  per sign and about 400 for the cover. Plan on roughly 15,000 credits.

  animate it in Roblox Studio or another editor using the timing plan above.
